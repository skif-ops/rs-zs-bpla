NetTie conversion finding
-------------------------
The KiCad footprint contains pads 1 and 2 plus a filled F.Cu fp_poly bridge and net_tie_pad_groups "1, 2". The initial Altium board import retained only the two pads in each NT1-NT3 component instance. A separate import produced NetTie.PcbLib with two pads plus one copper region. Altium matched only NT1-NT3 in an update ECO, but the board instances remained two-primitive components and the three unrouted reports remained. NT1-NT3 are component type Net Tie. L1 and L2 are component type Standard.

A test board-level track between the two differently named nets produced short-circuit and clearance violations and was undone. No equivalence claim or manufacturing release is made.
