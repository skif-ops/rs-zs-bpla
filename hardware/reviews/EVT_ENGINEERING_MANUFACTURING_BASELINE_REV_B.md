# EVT-PRE-20 engineering manufacturing baseline — Rev.B

PCB-MAIN COMPLETE-105 Rev.B is the accepted eight-layer EVT source. The former
six-layer baseline is superseded. PCB-PWR remains four-layer and PCB-MIC remains
two-layer. See `hardware/PCB_LAYER_COUNT_AUTHORITY_REV_B.csv` and
`hardware/PCB_MAIN_8L_ADOPTION_DECISION_REV_B.md`.

Manufacturing remains blocked until the board-specific DFM, selected stack,
impedance, POFV, Review B and first article gates are closed.
